The art of
paying attention

Reading is an invitation to slow down. In a world that rewards speed, a book asks us to be still long enough to notice something new.

There is a certain serendipity in turning a page and finding exactly the thought you did not know you needed. An unfamiliar word, a surprising idea, a sentence that stays with you.

Curiosity makes these small discoveries possible. Rather than rushing past what we do not understand, we can pause, look a little closer, and let the meaning unfold.

A good reading habit is not measured only in pages. It lives in the connections we make between the words on the page and the world beyond it.
